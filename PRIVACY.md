# Privacy Policy

**AISENS Alexa Skill**

Last updated: May 2026

## Data Collection

The AISENS skill does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Queries you make are processed in real time to retrieve web search results and are not retained after the session ends.

## Third-Party Services

Search results are retrieved via publicly available web sources. No data is transmitted to any third party for advertising or tracking purposes.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue at: https://github.com/joaquinphoenix-es/aisens-mcp-bridge/issues
